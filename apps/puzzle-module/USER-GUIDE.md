# Puzzle — User Guide

A personalized nine-piece photo puzzle you build for someone you love. They answer nine memory-based questions about your relationship — each correct answer reveals one piece of a photo. By the end, they've unlocked the whole picture, and a message from you.

This guide has two parts: one for the person creating the puzzle (the **Creator**), one for the person receiving it (the **Recipient**). If you're the Creator, you don't need to read the Recipient section — your recipient will figure it out on their own; there's nothing for them to set up.

---

## Part 1 — For Creators

### Getting started

1. Go to the sign-up page and create an account with your name, email, and a password (at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number) — or continue with Google.
2. You'll land on your **Dashboard**, which shows every puzzle you've made, grouped by Draft / Published / Completed.
3. Tap **Create New Puzzle** to start.

### Building your puzzle

The wizard walks you through six steps. Your progress saves automatically as you go — there's no "Save" button to remember, and you can leave and come back any time; your draft picks up right where you left off from the Dashboard.

**Step 1 — Occasion & Emotion.** Pick what you're celebrating (anniversary, birthday, proposal, wedding, and more) and the feeling you want the puzzle to carry.

**Step 2 — The photo.** Upload the photo that gets revealed piece by piece as they answer. You'll get a simple cropping tool to frame it into a square before it's sliced into nine pieces — drag to reposition, use the zoom controls or +/- keys to zoom in or out.

**Step 3 — Who it's for.** Enter your recipient's name and write a short welcome message they'll see before the first question — this is your chance to set the scene.

**Step 4 — Your nine questions.** This is the heart of the puzzle. For each of the nine pieces, write:
- A **question** about a shared memory ("Where did we go on our first date?").
- The **correct answer**.
- Up to **three optional clues** — hints that get more specific, in case they get stuck. You don't have to add any; it's fine to leave a question with no clues if you want it to be a pure test of memory. If you do add clues, they'll be revealed one at a time, only if asked for.

A tip: answers are matched fairly loosely (capitalization and extra spaces don't matter), but they still need to be close to what you typed — so if there are a couple of ways someone might phrase the answer, either accept a shorter, simpler version yourself, or use the clues to steer them toward the exact phrasing you had in mind.

**Step 5 — Wrapping it up.** Write two more things: a **partner-help message** — shown if your recipient uses up all of a question's clues and still can't get it, giving them a way to ask someone else (or you, in disguise) for help — and a **completion message**, shown once every piece is unlocked. Make this one count; it's the last thing they'll read.

**Step 6 — Review.** See everything you've built in one place, with a readiness badge (X / 9 ready) showing how many questions are complete. From here you can **Preview** the whole experience yourself, playing through it exactly as your recipient will — a good way to catch a typo or a question that's harder than you meant it to be — or **Publish** when you're happy with it.

### Publishing and sharing

Once you publish, you'll get:
- A **share link** — copy it and send it however you'd like (text, WhatsApp, email).
- A **QR code** — useful if you're handing this over in person, printed on a card, or displaying it at an event.
- A **Share via WhatsApp** button and, on supported devices, a native **Share…** button.

Send the link (or show the QR code) to your recipient, and they can open it right away — no account needed on their end.

### After publishing

- **You can't edit a puzzle's core details once your recipient has started playing.** Before they've opened the link and begun answering, you're free to revise it. Once they've started, the questions, photo, and other core content lock in place — this protects the experience from changing mid-way through for them.
- Your Dashboard shows each puzzle's status at a glance: **Draft** (still being built), **Published** (link is live, waiting), and **Completed** (your recipient finished it).

### A few things to know

- There's no way to see your recipient's individual answers or how many attempts they used — the puzzle is designed to feel like a private moment between the two of you, not something you watch over their shoulder.
- If you need a completely fresh start, create a new puzzle rather than trying to repurpose an old one — each is its own independent gift with its own link.

## Part 2 — For Recipients

### Opening your puzzle

Someone who cares about you sent you a link (or a QR code). Tap it — that's it. No account, no sign-up, no password. You'll land straight on a welcome screen with a short note from whoever made this for you.

Tap **Start the Puzzle** when you're ready.

### Playing

You'll see a 3×3 grid — nine locked tiles, each one a hidden piece of a photo. Tap any locked tile to open its question.

- Type your answer and submit it.
- **Correct?** That piece unlocks and you'll see part of the photo underneath, plus how many points you earned.
- **Not quite?** You'll get a chance to try again. If you're stuck, you can ask for a **clue** — up to three per question, each one more specific than the last.
- **Still stuck after every clue?** An "Ask Your Partner" option appears — a message the Creator wrote just for this moment, plus a one-tap way to message someone (over WhatsApp) and ask for help. Using it still unlocks the piece, just for fewer points than answering it yourself.

You can answer the nine questions in any order — tap whichever tile you'd like next.

Your progress bar tracks how many of the nine pieces you've unlocked and your running score.

### Finishing

Once all nine pieces are unlocked, you'll see the **full photo revealed**, your final score, a star rating, and a personal completion message written just for you.

### A few things to know

- **If you reload the page partway through**, your unlocked pieces and score are safe — they're saved as you go. The *text* of any clues you'd already revealed won't reappear after a reload (your clue count still shows correctly, you just won't see the words again) — if that happens, you can simply ask for the clue again where the option is still available, or try answering from memory.
- **The link is just for you.** It's not meant to be shared further or to show up anywhere public — treat it like the personal gift it is.
- **If a link says it's invalid or expired**, double check you copied the whole thing, and check with whoever sent it — it's possible the link was mistyped or something went wrong on their end when they shared it.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What's happening |
|---|---|
| "This link is invalid or has expired" | The share link is broken, mistyped, or points to a puzzle that no longer exists. Ask the Creator to re-send it. |
| A page shows an offline/connection banner | Your device lost its internet connection. Progress already made is safe — reconnect and continue; nothing is lost by a brief interruption. |
| A clue's text is missing after a reload | Expected, by design — see "A few things to know" above. Your progress and clue count are unaffected. |
| A Creator can't edit their puzzle anymore | The recipient has already started playing — core details lock at that point to protect their experience. Create a new puzzle if you need to send something different. |
